About Me

I am Akash Pandey, a multidisciplinary Business and Data Analyst who combines business intelligence, enterprise analytics, automation, artificial intelligence, and strategic problem-solving to help organizations convert complex information into clear and actionable decisions. I hold a Master of Science in Business Analytics from Wichita State University and am currently pursuing an additional graduate degree in Supply Chain Management, with a strong focus on analytics-driven operations, procurement, logistics, planning, and organizational optimization.

My professional strength lies in my ability to understand both the technical architecture behind data and the strategic business questions that data must answer. Rather than treating analytics as a reporting exercise, I approach it as a complete decision-support system. This means understanding organizational objectives, investigating source systems, validating data quality, designing scalable data models, automating repeatable processes, and presenting insights in a way that executives and operational teams can immediately understand and use.

In my current role as a Data Analyst at HFG Architecture, I work with enterprise information from Deltek Vantagepoint, SQL Server, Power BI, ODBC data connections, cloud services, and automated refresh pipelines. I have designed and developed executive dashboards covering financial performance, project profitability, workforce utilization, payroll analytics, employee realization, accounts receivable, forecasting, project transitions, and operational performance. My work involves more than building visuals—I investigate database structures, reconcile financial measures, create SQL views and stored procedures, automate refresh workflows, troubleshoot gateway issues, and transform fragmented information into dependable analytical systems.

I bring a rare combination of analytical depth, business understanding, technical adaptability, communication ability, and creative execution. My background spans business analytics, computer science, marketing strategy, web development, cybersecurity, research, digital communication, and leadership. This allows me to move comfortably between technical teams, business stakeholders, academic researchers, creative professionals, and organizational leadership.

My long-term professional direction is centered on building intelligent organizations—organizations in which data is trusted, processes are automated, decisions are evidence-based, and technology is aligned with real human and business needs. I am continuously developing my expertise in business intelligence, supply chain analytics, artificial intelligence, enterprise data systems, predictive modeling, process improvement, and strategic operations.

I do not simply analyze what has already happened. I work to understand why it happened, what it means, what may happen next, and what decision should follow. That combination of analytical thinking, technical execution, and strategic interpretation defines the value I bring to every project.

Research & Innovation

Theoretical Frameworks for AI-Driven Applications of NFC Tags in IoT Healthcare Monitoring and Predictive Marketing

My research examines how Near Field Communication, Internet of Things infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and predictive analytics can be combined to create more connected and proactive healthcare-monitoring environments.

The framework considers how NFC-enabled interactions could support patient identification, secure data access, wearable or connected health technologies, and intelligent analysis of health-related information. It also examines broader questions surrounding privacy, data security, ethical AI, scalability, accessibility, and responsible implementation.

This research was presented at the Kansas Data Science Conference and reflects my interest in designing technology that connects analytical capability with meaningful human outcomes.

Cricket and Its Origin in India

Completed a six-month research initiative examining the historical, social, and cultural development of cricket in India. The project explored how the sport evolved from its historical introduction into a powerful part of India's national identity, media environment, community life, and commercial ecosystem.
